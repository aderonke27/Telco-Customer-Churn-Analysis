## Project overview
AnalystLab Africa Consulting has been hired by ABC Communications Ltd, a telecom company, to study why customers leave. You’re the junior analyst. You take the Telco Customer Churn dataset (7,043 customers, 21 columns), which holds each customer’s demographics, subscribed services, contract and billing details, and a Churn column (Yes/No). You explore it, find patterns, and turn them into recommendations management can act on. The deliverables are a business understanding report, a dataset inspection report, a notebook or Excel workbook, and a presentation.

## Problem statement
ABC Communications is losing customers, and management doesn’t know who is leaving or why. Winning a new customer costs more than keeping an existing one, so every avoidable exit hurts revenue. The company needs data to show which customer groups, contracts, services and payment habits are linked to churn, so it can aim retention spending at the right people.

A one-line version for your report: “Identify the customer characteristics and behaviours associated with churn at ABC Communications, and recommend data-backed actions to reduce it.”

## Business questions
* What does the customer base look like? Look at gender, senior citizen status, partner and dependents, tenure, monthly charges, internet service type and contract type.
* What is the overall churn rate? This is the baseline for every segment comparison that follows.
* Which segments have the highest churn? Split Churn by senior citizen, partner, dependents, gender and internet service type.
* Does contract type influence retention? Compare churn across month-to-month, one-year and two-year contracts.
* Does tenure affect loyalty? Compare tenure for churned and retained customers using histograms, a box plot and tenure bands.
* Do higher monthly charges push customers out? Compare MonthlyCharges for churned and retained customers, and check the correlation heatmap.
* Which services influence churn? Check online security, tech support, online backup, device protection, streaming, phone service, multiple lines and internet service against churn.
* Which payment methods have higher churn? Compare electronic check, mailed check, bank transfer and credit card, and look at paperless billing too.
* What actions should management take? Turn the findings from questions 1 to 8 into at least five recommendations.
